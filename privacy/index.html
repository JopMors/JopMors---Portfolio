Privacy.

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This website (jopmors.com) is my personal portfolio. I use no cookies, no trackers and no analytics. The only place personal data is processed is the contact form.

What I process

If you fill in the contact form, your name, email address and message are sent to Formspree, the service that handles these messages for me and forwards them to my email. I use this information only to reply to your message.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub privacy statement for details. Everything else, including fonts and scripts, is served from this site itself.

How long I keep it

I keep messages no longer than needed to answer your question, and delete the email conversation once it is no longer relevant.

Sharing with others

I do not share your data with anyone, except Formspree as the necessary processor for the contact form.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (AVG) you can ask to see, correct or delete your data. Email me at jop.mors@yahoo.nl. You can also file a complaint with the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Questions

Questions about this statement? Send me an email or a message on LinkedIn.
